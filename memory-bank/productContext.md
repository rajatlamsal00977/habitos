# Product Context

## Why this exists

Users drop habits because difficulty, app overload, and fading motivation create **pressure**. Bloom reduces pressure, supports emotionally, and makes **tiny, repeatable** actions the hero.

## Experience principles

- **Calm, modern, emotionally safe** — whitespace, soft gradients, supportive copy
- **Identity-first** — “Who do you want to become?” drives onboarding and framing
- **Today only** on home — minimize overwhelm; one-thumb, low typing
- **Soft gamification** — streaks and rewards feel **cozy**, not competitive (glow, mascot evolution, gentle motion)

## Key flows (PRD)

1. **Onboarding:** identity → “what would they do daily?” → tiny habit suggestions → reminder time, cue, energy level
2. **Home:** greeting, avatar, streak, points; mascot + microcopy; max 3 highlighted habits with large rounded cards; floating glass-style tab bar
3. **Habit creation:** conversational, one question per screen, smooth transitions
4. **After completion:** “How do you feel?” (worse / same / better) for future insights
5. **Low energy:** one tap to shrink requirements while preserving streak momentum

## Success metrics (prioritize)

- Daily retention, streak continuation, emotional positivity trend, habit consistency — **not** raw task counts

## `ui.json` alignment

- **Design system name:** Calm Habit Growth Mobile UI
- **Feel:** pastel wellness, soft gradients, mascot-driven, single primary action per screen, thumb-friendly targets
- **Mascot:** 3D soft, rounded, low-detail, expressive — idle, celebrate, comfort on misses
- **Motion:** springs, soft easing, card float, completion micro-bounce, glow on streak — avoid harsh or flashy motion
